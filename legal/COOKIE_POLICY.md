## 1. What we use

| Cookie / storage | Type | Purpose | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `asculto_session` | strictly necessary | auth session | not required |
| `asculto_api_key` (localStorage) | functional | remember your API key in the SPA | not required (user action) |
| Analytics | analytics | aggregate usage | **required** (off by default) |

## 2. Consent
We set non-essential cookies only after you consent via the banner, and you can
withdraw at any time. Strictly-necessary cookies are always on.

## 3. Managing cookies
Use the in-app consent control or your browser settings to delete/block cookies.
