# Asculto Data Processing Addendum (draft)

_This DPA forms part of the agreement between [CUSTOMER] ("Controller") and
[LEGAL ENTITY] ("Processor") for the Asculto service._

> **Not legal advice.** GDPR-first. Complete the annexes before signing.

## 1. Roles and scope
Processor processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from the
Controller and only to provide the service. The subject matter, duration, nature,
and purpose of processing are set out in Annex 1.

## 2. Processor obligations
The Processor shall: (a) process only on instructions; (b) ensure personnel
confidentiality; (c) implement the technical and organisational measures in
Annex 2; (d) assist with data-subject requests and DPIAs; (e) notify the
Controller without undue delay (within [48] hours) of a personal-data breach;
(f) delete or return data on termination; (g) make available audit information.

## 3. Sub-processors
The Controller grants general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in
Annex 3 (Cloudflare, Neon, Modal, Stripe, and any LLM provider). The Processor
will give prior notice of changes and the Controller may object.

## 4. International transfers
Transfers outside the EEA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where
required, a Transfer Impact Assessment. EU data residency is the default.

## 5. Security
See Annex 2: encryption at rest/in transit, access control, signed URLs,
segregation of environments, secret management, and monitoring.

## 6. Liability and term
Liability is subject to the limitations in the main agreement. This DPA continues
while the Processor processes Personal Data.
